The Problem We Observed

As a first-year B.Com (Hons) student at Shaheed Bhagat Singh College, I noticed a recurring issue across campus — students and staff frequently misplace important belongings such as ID cards, wallets, phones, books, and more.

Usually, people post about lost or found items in various WhatsApp groups or chat platforms. However, the biggest issue is that not everyone is part of the same group. This communication gap often prevents lost items from reaching their rightful owners.

The Solution

To bridge this communication gap, I developed a centralized Lost & Found website exclusively for SBSC students and staff.

This platform allows:

  • Users to report lost items.
  • Finders to list recovered items.
  • Owners to browse and contact the finder directly.
One single trusted platform instead of scattered group messages.

AI-Powered Smart Matching

What makes this platform unique is its AI-powered matching system. The system analyzes item descriptions using intelligent algorithms and automatically identifies strong matches between lost and found items.

When a high similarity match is detected, the system shares contact details with both parties so they can quickly connect and recover the item.

Building a Stronger SBSC Community

This open-source initiative is built exclusively for the SBSC community. Our goal is to increase the chances of recovering lost belongings while building a more connected and supportive campus environment.

The platform will continue to evolve with new features, improved AI accuracy, enhanced security, and better user experience.
